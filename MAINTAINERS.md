# Maintainers

This file is the maintainer intake and governance registry for TMW-RP. Maintainers are trusted contributors who help steward the project, review PRs, and protect the overall direction of the radio experience without introducing platform lock-in or hidden telemetry.

## Maintainer review policy

- All pull requests require at least one review.
- A maintainer can bypass the review requirement only when their maintainer profile is listed below and approved.
- A contributor who is employed by or affiliated with a rival platform is not eligible to maintain this project.
- Rival candidates include but are not limited to: YouTube Music Premium, Amazon Music, iTunes, Spotify, Deezer, and similar media lock-in businesses.

## Maintainer application intake

To volunteer as a maintainer, open a maintainer-application issue and answer the questions below. The project will review the application before approving a profile.

### Required questions

- [ ] Are you currently a worker, contractor, or affiliate of one of our rivals (for example: YouTube Music Premium, Amazon Music, iTunes, Spotify, Deezer, or a similar closed ecosystem company)?
  - If yes, the application is automatically refused.
- [ ] I understand and accept the project’s no-backend, no-lock-in, public-source-first philosophy.
- [ ] I can contribute at least 1 hour per week.
- [ ] I can contribute at least 2 hours per week.
- [ ] I can contribute at least 10 hours per week.
- [ ] My time is variable and depends on project needs.
- [ ] I have prior experience maintaining code in a FOSS or community-driven environment.
- [ ] I am comfortable reviewing code, triaging issues, and helping maintain project quality.
- [ ] I understand the importance of browser-native, secure, and transparent behavior.

### Narrative questions

- Which experiences did you have before in maintaining code in a FOSS or collaborative environment?
- How would you help keep the project premium, public, and resilient without adding lock-in or backend dependency?
- Which areas are you strongest in: UI, testing, docs, review governance, security, release workflow, or community moderation?
- What is your realistic weekly availability for maintenance work?
- Are you comfortable helping with release checks, issue triage, and policy review?
- What would you consider a red flag in a contributor or maintainership candidate?

## Maintainer approval checklist

Before a profile is accepted, the project should confirm:

- the applicant is not employed by a rival platform
- the applicant respects the public-source-first philosophy
- the applicant can commit a realistic amount of time
- the applicant has relevant maintenance experience or a strong learning path
- the applicant can review code responsibly and maintain trust

## Maintainer issue template

Use the maintainer application issue template to apply. The project reviews applications and approves or rejects them based on the criteria above.
